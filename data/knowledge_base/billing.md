# Billing Support

## Billing Cycle

Customers are charged according to their selected billing plan. The billing date may vary depending on when the subscription was started.

## Unexpected Charge

If a customer notices an unexpected charge, they should review their billing history and verify whether the charge is related to an active subscription or recent purchase.

If the charge cannot be identified, the customer should contact support with the transaction details.

## Duplicate Charge

If a customer believes they were charged twice for the same transaction, they should check their payment history first. If two completed charges are present for the same purchase, they should contact support with the transaction details.

## Invoice

Customers can request or access their invoice through their account billing section. If an invoice is missing or contains incorrect information, they should contact customer support.

## Billing Information

Customers who need to update their billing information should use the billing settings available in their account. If they cannot update the information, they should contact customer support.

## Subscription Renewal

Subscriptions may renew automatically according to the customer's selected plan. Customers should review their subscription settings to understand their renewal date and billing amount.

## Billing Dispute

Customers who believe a billing charge is incorrect should contact customer support and provide the relevant transaction information so the issue can be investigated.
