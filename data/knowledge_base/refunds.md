# Refund Support

## Refund Eligibility

Refund eligibility depends on the type of purchase and the applicable refund policy. Customers should contact support if they are unsure whether a purchase qualifies for a refund.

## Request a Refund

Customers who want to request a refund should contact customer support and provide the relevant order or transaction details.

Support will review the request and determine whether the transaction qualifies for a refund.

## Refund Processing

After a refund is approved, the processing time may depend on the payment method and financial institution.

Customers should allow the required processing time before contacting support about a missing refund.

## Refund Not Received

If a customer has been informed that a refund was processed but the amount has not appeared, they should check their payment account and allow the expected processing period.

If the refund is still missing after that period, they should contact support with the refund or transaction details.

## Partial Refund

A partial refund may be issued when only part of an order or service qualifies for a refund.

Customers should contact support if they believe the refunded amount is incorrect.

## Cancelled Order Refund

If an order is cancelled and qualifies for a refund, the refund will be processed according to the applicable refund policy.

Customers should retain their order information until the refund has been completed.

## Refund Status

Customers can contact support to check the status of a refund. They should provide the relevant order or transaction information so the request can be located.
